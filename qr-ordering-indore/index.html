How it works

From the table to your kitchen in seconds

  1. Guest scans the table QR

    Any phone camera opens your menu. No download, no sign-up.

  2. Picks dishes and adds notes

    Veg marks, prices and notes like "less spicy", on the guest's own phone.

  3. Kitchen token prints

    The order appears on your counter screen and the token prints for the chef.

  4. Bill at the end

    Cash, card or your own UPI. A GST bill prints at the counter.
