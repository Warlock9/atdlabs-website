FAQ

Questions restaurant owners ask

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the table QR with their phone camera and the menu opens in the browser. No download and no sign-up.

Do you take a commission on my orders?

No. You pay a small monthly fee. Every rupee from your sales goes to your own UPI, card machine or counter.

How do guests pay?

The way they do today: cash, card or your own UPI at the end of the meal. A GST bill prints at the counter.

Which printer do I need?

A standard 80mm thermal printer prints kitchen tokens and GST bills. If you already have one, we set it up for you.

Who sets up my menu?

We do. Send a photo of your printed menu and we load the items and prices, and print QR codes for your tables.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The first 30 days are free, including setup.

Do you work outside my city?

Yes. We work with businesses all over India, on-site or online.
